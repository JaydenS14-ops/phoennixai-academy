# Rise to Capital and Admin Password Validation Notes

The protected `/admin` sign-in page now presents a password field with an accessible eye control. The field loads as `type="password"` with the control labelled “Show password”; activating it changes the field to `type="text"` and the control label to “Hide password”, without submitting credentials.

The Rise to Capital page exposes a public high-level curriculum overview, three certification pathways, a careful progression note for CPD and investor-network opportunities, and enquiry conversion links. The presentation avoids representing accreditation, investor introductions, or investment outcomes as guaranteed.
