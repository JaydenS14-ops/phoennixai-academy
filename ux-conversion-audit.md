# UX and Conversion Audit

## Journey findings and applied refinements

The public website now offers a focused first-step conversion path from the homepage, programme catalogue, Rise to Capital page, curriculum specification, and header navigation. The homepage keeps a primary “Begin enquiry” action and a secondary exploration action, avoiding excessive competing calls to action. Rise to Capital has been added as a direct option in the existing intake form, reducing the distance between founder interest and a recorded enquiry.

The header now provides clear routes to programmes, curriculum, Rise to Capital, events, the mandate, and the protected Admin Management Centre. At narrower widths these destinations remain available through the mobile navigation menu. The animated partner strip is intentionally placed immediately after the homepage hero, so partner credibility appears before a visitor needs to search for it.

The Rise to Capital curriculum uses a 15-module expandable interface, reducing cognitive load compared with a full static course outline while leaving the complete scope transparent. The PhoennixAI Academy and Agency specification gives visitors a single page explaining how skill development, product work, quality assurance, and AI automation connect.

The floating back-to-top control is positioned at the lower left and the visitor-support launcher at the lower right. This preserves easy access to both actions without overlap. Motion uses transitions and respects reduced-motion preferences; the partner marquee pauses on hover to keep its content inspectable.

## Validation

Desktop and mobile review covered the homepage, Rise to Capital, curriculum specification, and intake flow. The display maintained readable hierarchy, distinct conversion routes, responsive card arrangements, and unobstructed form controls. Automated tests and TypeScript validation passed after the changes.
