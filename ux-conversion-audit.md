# UX and Conversion Audit

## Journey findings and applied refinements

The public website now offers a focused first-step conversion path from the homepage, programme catalogue, Rise to Capital page, curriculum specification, and header navigation. The primary action now says **“Find your pathway”**, which better describes the next step for young learners, parents and guardians, adult learners, and PhoennixAI Agency applicants.

The former child-only student intake has been replaced by a pathway enquiry. Visitors identify themselves as a parent or guardian, adult learner, work-experience applicant, or Agency apprenticeship applicant before entering contact details. The form then adjusts its age guidance, field labels, opportunity list, availability prompt, confirmation message, and server validation to the chosen route. This reduces the chance that adult and Agency visitors abandon the form because the terminology does not describe them.

The header now provides clear routes to programmes, curriculum, Rise to Capital, events, the mandate, and the protected Admin Management Centre. Its full navigation appears at common laptop widths rather than being delayed until an extra-wide display; at narrower widths the destinations remain available through the mobile navigation menu. The animated partner strip is intentionally placed immediately after the homepage hero, so partner credibility appears before a visitor needs to search for it.

The Rise to Capital curriculum uses a 15-module expandable interface, reducing cognitive load compared with a full static course outline while leaving the complete scope transparent. The PhoennixAI Academy and Agency specification gives visitors a single page explaining how skill development, product work, quality assurance, and AI automation connect.

The floating back-to-top control is positioned at the lower left and the visitor-support launcher at the lower right. This preserves easy access to both actions without overlap. The visitor-support prompts now describe adult learning and Agency opportunities alongside Academy programmes. Motion uses transitions and respects reduced-motion preferences; the partner marquee pauses on hover to keep its content inspectable.

The In Motion archive now has accessible, visible filters for **Events**, **Alumni**, **Founders**, and **Retreats**, as well as an “All” reset. Empty filter states explain that no matching stories are published yet and offer a one-click return to all moments. Public archive messaging no longer exposes internal administrative wording.

The Innovation in Practice image is a supporting brochure photograph for the “Courage to imagine. Discipline to build.” section. It is intended to visualise collaborative studio learning and is now confirmed to render correctly in the public experience.

## Validation

Desktop and mobile review covered the homepage, inclusive pathway enquiry, In Motion archive, Rise to Capital, and curriculum specification. The display maintained readable hierarchy, distinct conversion routes, responsive card arrangements, working studio imagery, accessible archive filters, and unobstructed form controls. Automated tests and TypeScript validation passed after the changes.
